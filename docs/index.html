Hello! I’m Minh. I study hyperbolic surfaces and their length spectra.

  • PhD (2018–2022) at the University of Luxembourg, where Hugo Parlier supervised my thesis.
  • Master’s (2016–2017) at the University of Nice Sophia Antipolis, supervised by François Labourie.
  • BA in Mathematics (2013–2015) at the University of Education, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
