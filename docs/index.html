Nhat Minh Doan (Đoàn Nhật Minh)

Hello! I am Minh, a researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, VAST. I was a SASEA Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore under Prof. Ser Peow Tan. My PhD thesis was completed at the University of Luxembourg under Prof. Hugo Parlier in 2022.

My research focuses on hyperbolic surfaces, length spectra, and related topics. I was introduced to hyperbolic geometry during my Master’s (M2) at the University of Nice Sophia Antipolis in 2017, where Prof. François Labourie supervised my master’s thesis.
