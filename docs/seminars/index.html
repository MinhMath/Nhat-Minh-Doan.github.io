Online Seminars

Hyperbolic Geometry & Related Topics Online Seminar

I organize this online seminar for researchers and graduate students to share current work, discuss new ideas, and build connections across institutions. Topics include hyperbolic geometry, Teichmüller theory, low-dimensional topology, geometric group theory, and dynamics.

If you would like to give a talk, please email me a title, a short abstract, and your availability, including your time zone.
